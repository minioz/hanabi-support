Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This policy applies to Hanabi – Japanese Vocab.

Data and Privacy

Hanabi works entirely offline. The app does not collect or transmit personal information, require an account, or use advertising, tracking, or analytics.

Learning progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes this local data. Copies may remain in device backups according to your Apple backup settings.

Support Emails

If you email us, we use your email address and the information you provide to respond to your request. These communications are processed by our email provider and retained as needed to provide support. You may contact us to request deletion.

Changes

If the app’s privacy practices change, we will update this policy and the date above.
